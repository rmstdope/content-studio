## The fine-tuned-take test in imageTakes failed two full local gates in a row

**What happened.** After a one-comment commit, `bun run check` failed twice running on
`src/services/imageTakes.test.ts`, "adding a fine-tuned take > adds the next take with its
picture and what it came from, made from the image's words now" (`board.takes[1]` undefined), and
passed on the third run. The branch does not touch that file. Run alone 15 times it failed in 4,
once together with "copies the words and style of the take it came from". Load average was 9 to 16.

**Why.** Not established here. as-mirh and as-xmde read it as the test calling `addTake` without
waiting for the take that `make` started.

**Cost.** Two extra full gate runs, about six minutes, and the 15 runs of the file.

**Prevent by.** A bead for the fix as-mirh names: in "adding a fine-tuned take",
`await settled('inn')` after each `make` that is followed by `addTake`. The retrospectives have
named it repeatedly and no open bead matching "flaky", "imageTakes" or "fine-tuned take" carries
it; filing one is the navigator's call.
