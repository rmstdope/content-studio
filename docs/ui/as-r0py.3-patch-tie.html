Agreed (as-r0py.3): tying an effect to a patch. Every effect that is on has a “Shows” menu under its sliders: “Always”, or “While <patch> is laid” for one of the image's patches. It is saved at once and goes to the game. A tied effect carries a tag beside its name, on or off. Tying lays that patch in the preview. If the patch is switched off later, the sliders dim and “Lay it” brings it back. A patch with no take chosen can be tied to. A patch the game no longer lists keeps its tie, flagged.
