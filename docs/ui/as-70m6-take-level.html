How it behaves (decided, so it matches the page today)

  • The slider moves in steps of 5%, from 10% to 200%. The arrow keys move it by 5%; it saves half a second after the last key press, or when you let go of the mouse.
  • Moving it while the take plays changes the loudness straight away. Play, “The chosen, then this”, Hear the seam and the Fades editor all play the take at its level.
  • The waveform's bars grow and shrink with the level.
  • While Fades… is open on a take, its slider is hidden with the rest of the card, as Trim silence is today.
  • Each take keeps its own level after a reload. Choosing a take puts it into the game at its level; un-choosing and choosing again keeps the level.
  • The level on the Scenes page still applies on top of this one.
