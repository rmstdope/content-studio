Clean up the voice — agreed

Every state of the clean-up in Malakar’s Voice panel. Play loops the chosen line until you stop it. Clicking either half of “As recorded | Cleaned” flips it, and the sound switches over at the same moment in the line. Every word here is as it would ship.
