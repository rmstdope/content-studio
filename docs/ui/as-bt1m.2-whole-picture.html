The agreed whole-picture mark (as-bt1m.2) — “mark the whole picture” (or ⌘A, Ctrl+A off a Mac, while not typing in the words) sits in the line under the words on Fine-tune and Patches, where the mark is explained and cleared. A whole-picture mark works as a patch does: the model sees the whole picture and only what it changed is kept; in Fine-tune, no mark still paints the whole picture again.
