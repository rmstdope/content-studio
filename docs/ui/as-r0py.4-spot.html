as-r0py.4: drawing an effect's spot. "Draw a spot" (or "Change") on an effect's row opens drawing on the tab's picture: the slow zoom stops and the other effects pause, while the effect being drawn plays only inside its shapes. Click the corners, and click the first corner to close a shape. Drag a corner to move it, double-click one to take it out, and click a shape to pick it. "＋ Add a shape" starts another shape. Every closed shape and moved corner is saved at once, and "Finish" leaves drawing. Pointing at a row outlines its spot. With no spot the effect plays over the whole picture; "Clear" goes back to that. Drawing happens on the tab only; full screen plays the spots.
